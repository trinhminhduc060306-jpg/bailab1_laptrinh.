
. Noi dung va cach thuc thuc hien

Cau 1: Nhap thong tin ca nhan
Su dung `Console.ReadLine()` de nhap ho ten va nam sinh. Tinh tuoi bang cach lay nam hien tai tru nam sinh, sau do hien thi ket qua.

Cau 2: Tinh toan hinh tru
Nhap ban kinh va chieu cao bang kieu `double`. Kiem tra du lieu phai lon hon 0, sau do ap dung cong thuc tinh dien tich xung quanh va the tich.

Cau 3: Xep loai hoc tap
Nhap diem trung binh, kiem tra diem nam trong khoang tu 0 den 10. Su dung `if-else` de xac dinh muc xep loai tuong ung.

Cau 4: Menu va vong lap
Su dung `do-while` de hien thi menu lap lai, `switch-case` de xu ly lua chon va `for` de tinh tong hoac in bang cuu chuong. Lua chon 0 dung chuong trinh.

Cau 5: Xu ly ngoai le phep chia
Nhap hai so nguyen va thuc hien phep chia. Su dung `try-catch-finally` de xu ly loi nhap sai, chia cho 0 va hien thi thong bao ket thuc.

Cau 6: Giai phuong trinh bac hai
Nhap ba he so a, b, c. Kiem tra truong hop a bang 0, tinh Delta = b² - 4ac va dua vao Delta de xac dinh so nghiem. Hien thi nghiem voi hai chu so thap phan.

Cau 7: Thong ke day so
Nhap n va tao mang mot chieu de luu n so nguyen. Duyet mang bang vong lap de tinh tong, tim gia tri lon nhat, nho nhat va dem so chan. Tinh trung binh bang tong chia cho n.

Cau 8: Kiem tra so nguyen to
Duyet cac so tu 2 den n. Voi moi so, kiem tra kha nang chia het cho cac so tu 2 den can bac hai cua no. Neu khong co uoc nao trong khoang nay, do la so nguyen to.

Cau 9: Doc so tu 0 den 99
Tach so thanh hang chuc bang phep chia nguyen `/ 10` va hang don vi bang phep chia lay du `% 10`. Su dung `switch-case` de doc tung phan va xu ly cac truong hop dac biet.

Cau 10: Ve hinh bang ky tu
Su dung menu `switch-case` de chon hinh va cac vong lap `for` long nhau de in dau `*`. Ket hop cau truc `if-else` de xac dinh vi tri can in dau sao hoac khoang trang, tao tam giac dac, tam giac rong va hinh vuong rong.

3. Cong nghe su dung

4. Cach chay chuong trinh
1. Mo file `IT3212_Labs.slnx` bang Visual Studio.
2. Chon `Lab01_Console` lam Startup Project.
3. Nhan `Ctrl + F5` de chay chuong trinh.
4. Nhap du lieu theo yeu cau va kiem tra ket qua hien thi.

5. Ket qua dat duoc
Thong qua cac bai tap, nguoi hoc ren luyen ky nang nhap xuat du lieu, su dung bien va mang, cau truc dieu kien, vong lap, switch-case, ham va xu ly ngoai le.
